Questions

What is a crypto prop firm?

A crypto prop firm gives traders an account for cryptocurrency markets and pays a share of the profits under its rules. Some run crypto CFD accounts on platforms such as Match-Trader, DXtrade or MT5, while others run perpetual futures programs on exchange-style platforms. You usually pass an evaluation first or buy an instant account, then trade within daily loss and drawdown limits.

What is a crypto perps prop firm program?

Perpetual futures, or perps, are crypto contracts with no expiry date that track the spot price. Some prop firms run programs on perps, often through exchange-connected platforms, with their own rules on leverage, drawdown and payouts. Prop Firm Mirror shows perps programs in their own section, so you can compare them separately from crypto CFD programs.

How do I compare crypto prop firms?

Compare the program, not the brand: profit target, daily loss limit, maximum drawdown and whether it is static or trailing, profit split, payout schedule, leverage, platform and the price for your account size. Then check whether your country is restricted. The table on this page puts the key rules side by side, and each firm's page links the official source for every rule.

Can I hold crypto trades over the weekend at a prop firm?

Crypto markets trade every day, and many crypto programs allow weekend holding, but each firm sets its own rule, sometimes with a maximum holding time or different rules for the evaluation and the funded account. Check the weekend rule quoted for each program on the firm's page, or filter to programs that allow weekend holding in the interactive comparison.

Are crypto prop firms available in every country?

No. Each firm publishes its own restricted-country list, and lists usually cover sanctioned countries, with many firms adding others. Prop Firm Mirror shows each list exactly as the firm publishes it, with the source and date, or says when no list is published. A country missing from a list does not guarantee acceptance, since payment and KYC checks can still differ.
