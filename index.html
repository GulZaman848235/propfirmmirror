Compare prop firms side by side

Prop Firm Mirror compares 70 prop trading firms across forex and CFDs, futures, crypto and perpetuals. For every firm you see the challenge prices, profit targets, daily loss and maximum drawdown rules, profit split, payout schedule, consistency rules, news and weekend rules, platforms and restricted countries, each taken from the firm's own website with a link to the page and the date we checked it.

How we verify every prop firm

We read each firm's own pages, help centre and terms, never review blogs or affiliate sites. Data is re-checked every day and every firm at least every three days; rule changes are logged with the date the firm gave. If a firm does not publish something, we say so instead of guessing.

Mirror Score

Each firm gets a Mirror Score, our opinion built from its payout record (35%), public reviews from many platforms (35%), its rules and costs (18%) and its track record (12%). The score changes when the evidence changes.
