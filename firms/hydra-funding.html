Good to know

  • Instant-funding only: no challenges or phases. Per the firm's 19 Oct 2026 post, it opened in 2022 selling challenges, launched futures in April 2025 and stopped selling challenges in March 2026.
  • One 'Unified Hydra Ruleset' across Forex, Crypto and Futures; tiers differ only in profit buffer, Best Day rule and inactivity period.
  • Every account keeps the rules it was purchased under.
  • Combined account size cap of $500,000 at any one time.
  • 'Double Your Account': after 3 successful withdrawals within 180 days from the same Instant account (each at least 5% of starting balance, no breaches), a bonus account of the same size, capped at $100,000.
  • All accounts are simulated. Futures demo access is listed as 'coming soon'.
  • The FAQ also lists PropX (Forex, Crypto) and the homepage lists GooeY PRO; neither is in the standard platform list.

What we couldn't verify

  • The brief said the FAQ shows no restricted-country list, but on 2026-10-30 the FAQ had one (read twice, 11 countries including Pakistan), and the Terms of Use point to it.
  • Futures platforms are inconsistent: the FAQ lists DxFutures and Volumetrica, but its platform-fee entry names Tradovate, DXTrade or Volumetrica.
  • Crypto: the FAQ does not say whether instruments are perpetual futures or spot (perps set false, marked unverified); crypto leverage is 1:2 in the FX list but 5:1 BTC/ETH and 2:1 others in the crypto section.
  • EAs and bots are not addressed directly in the FAQ.
  • No maximum withdrawal is stated in the FAQ (the $400K Lite post says 'no maximum').
