Good to know

  • Futures only (CME, CBOT, NYMEX, COMEX); forex, CFDs, stocks, options and crypto not offered.
  • Evaluation, Pro and S2F accounts are simulated; S2L leads to a live account with firm capital and an 80/20 split.
  • Up to 15 simulated funded accounts (Pro + S2F combined; max 5 EOD Pro and max 5 S2F). Evaluations unlimited. Up to 5 live accounts, one tradeable at a time.
  • Moving from Pro/S2F to live is by invitation at the firm's discretion; simulated accounts are closed once live and excess simulated balances are generally forfeited.
  • Simulated programs (incl. S2L) are closed to professionals (Series licence holders, SEC/NFA-registered). One login per household; must be 18+.
  • Commissions per contract round turn on simulated accounts: $3.98 equity minis, $1.02 equity micros/MCL, $1.52 MGC, $4.62 metals, $3.96 CL/NG/HO/RB, $4.72 currency, $5.58 agricultural.
  • Add-ons: second login session $30/month, Level 2 all exchanges $30/month (one exchange $10/month), rTrader Pro copier $10/month.
  • No refunds on evaluations, rebills, activation fees or add-ons. No evaluation resets.
  • Martingale, hedging and HFT are prohibited (Trading Rules collection).
