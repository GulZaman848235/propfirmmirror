nordicfunder.com publishes no country list. Checked the homepage, /faq/, /legal/terms/ and /support/. The terms page is a draft ('Awaiting counsel sign-off'): it says the full terms still sit in 'the current client terms document', which is hosted by a third party at dashboardanalytix.com/client-terms-and-policies/. That document also has no country list, only a right to limit access to any geographic region or jurisdiction. The current FAQ no longer mentions country eligibility. The footer names different assessment providers on different pages (Prop Account, LLC vs Forest Park FX LTD), so the site looks mid-rebuild. Pakistan's status is not stated.

Good to know

  • Platforms are integrated via GooeyTrade; the platform is chosen at checkout.
  • Assessments are provided through an affiliated entity (Forest Park FX LTD / Prop Account, LLC), the same Prop Account group that appears in Ment Funding's terms.
  • No deadline to hit targets; inactivity limit is 30 days on staged FX and 14 days on crypto, equities and instant funding.

What we couldn't verify

  • No restricted-country list published.
  • Legal entity unclear: pages name both Forest Park FX LTD and Prop Account, LLC; no address. Terms page says the operative terms are awaiting counsel sign-off.
  • Standard FX One/Two/Three-Step pages show no payout schedule; the FAQ's 'no delay, then every 14 days' is applied but not confirmed per track.
  • News-trading rules not stated anywhere on the site.
  • Crypto contract type (CFD vs perpetual) and per-track platform not stated.
