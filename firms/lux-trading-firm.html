Good to know

  • A stop-loss must be placed before every trade; missing stop-losses are rule breaches.
  • Funded accounts scale by stages after each 10% gain, up to $10,000,000 for the 1-Step Evaluation.
  • Lux states funded-stage accounts are executed on an A-book model; INSTA accounts start on demo.
  • Reset fees: £139 (100K), £299 (400K), £699 (1M) evaluations; £209 / £549 INSTA.
  • Prediction market entries allowed only between $0.10 and $0.90 contract prices; hedging banned.

What we couldn't verify

  • No restricted-country list published; T&C refer to a 'declaration of compliance' that was not found.
  • Daily loss limit, weekend holding and leverage not stated in the current (new) trading rules.
  • $1M evaluation price differs between the rules page (£999) and the homepage (£699).
  • Founding year not stated (copyright '2020-26' only).
  • Prediction Markets news/event rules and payout minimum not stated.
