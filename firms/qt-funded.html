Good to know

  • Maximum total funded allocation $300,000 per trader, of which instant accounts max $100,000.
  • QT Futures is listed as 'coming soon' and is not sold yet.
  • Older plans (QT 2 Step, QT 2 Step Elite, old Instant Funded) are marked discontinued in the help centre.
  • US residents cannot use MT5 (MetaQuotes policy); connecting from a US IP to MT5 is a hard breach.
  • Quant Tekel (Pty) Ltd is a separate FSCA-authorised broker in South Africa; QTFunded itself states it offers simulated programs only.
