Good to know

  • Instant Funding acquired Funded Trading Plus in May 2026 (per the firm's help centre).
  • The firm does not offer futures.
  • A per-instrument symbol loss limit is a hard-breach rule (3% on 2-Step Classic, based on the prior day's balance at 16:59 EST).
  • Margin use above recommended levels (60% on $100K accounts) triggers a manual risk review at pass, scaling and payout milestones.
  • Hedging on the same account is allowed; hedging across accounts is prohibited.
  • Programs are swap-free. Payout review usually takes up to 2 business days; KYC and a signed trader agreement are required.
  • The help centre also lists legacy programs (Experienced, Advanced, Premium Trader); they are not listed here as current programs.

What we couldn't verify

  • 1-Step Express and Instant consistency rules are not stated on their program pages.
  • Activation Account: profit split, payout schedule, EA policy and leverage after activation are not published in its help articles.
  • Minimum trading days are not stated for 1-Step Express, 2-Step Classic or Instant.
  • Help-centre dates use MM.DD.YYYY; '07.02.2026' was read as 2 July 2026.
