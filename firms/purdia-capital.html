Good to know

  • Path: Evaluation or Instant Funded account -> Sim Funded Account (SFA) -> Temporary SFA or Live Funded Account (LFA). Evaluation and SFA accounts are not payout-eligible; payouts from a Temp SFA reduce the capital that can transfer to the LFA.
  • LFA is a live brokerage account with a static drawdown: minimum balance fixed at breakeven + $100 of the transferred balance.
  • Account cap: 3 accounts total across evaluations, SFAs, Temp SFAs and LFAs combined. One user account per household.
  • Trading days and profitable days are counted midnight to midnight US Eastern Time, so one futures session can span two Purdia trading days.
  • Live account position limits are set with the assigned risk manager.

  • purdia.com is fully script-rendered: every page fetched (home, programs, knowledge base, terms, FAQ, checkout) returned only the page title. All rules and prices come from search-engine snippets of official purdia.com URLs, so most program fields are marked unverified.
