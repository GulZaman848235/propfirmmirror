RZR Funded (formerly Hantec Trader) lists 33 restricted countries.

Afghanistan Australia Belgium Bulgaria Congo Czechia DR Congo Egypt Germany Haiti Iran Israel Jordan Kosovo Laos Libya Malaysia Myanmar North Korea Pakistan Puerto Rico Qatar Romania Russia Serbia Somalia South Sudan Taiwan Thailand United States Uzbekistan Vietnam Yemen

The official help-centre article (Freshdesk, linked from rzrfunded.com, 'Modified on Mon, 5 Oct' = 2026-10-05) lists 33 countries. It includes Pakistan, Australia, Germany, Belgium, Czech Republic, Bulgaria, Romania, Malaysia, Thailand, Taiwan, Vietnam, Puerto Rico and the United States (the site footer also says it serves no US residents or citizens). Congo (Brazzaville) = CG; Congo (Kinshasa) = CD. The Terms and the site footer also say Instant programs (Instant24, Instant Lite, Instant Funding) are not available to residents of the UK, Mauritius, Hong Kong and Singapore. Challenges stay open there, so GB, MU, HK and SG are moved out of the code list (see removed) and should stay as an Instant-only note. Nationals or residents of UN, EU and OFAC-sanctioned countries are also excluded; those countries are not named. Pakistan (PK) IS now restricted; our old note said Pakistan was not nam
