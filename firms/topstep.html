Trading Combine (Standard path)

Model1-Step · Futures
Account sizes and list prices$50K: $49/mo · $100K: $99/mo · $150K: $199/mo
Price noteMonthly subscription, rebills every 30 days. One-time $149 activation fee per Express Funded Account (XFA) earned. Reset costs the same as the monthly price. Level 2 data $38/month optional. Path cannot be changed after purchase.
BillingMonthly subscription
Profit targetTrading Combine: $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 (50K / 100K / 150K)
Daily loss limitOptional: $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 if added at purchase; hitting it flattens positions and pauses trading until the next session (not a rule violation). Live Funded Account always has one.
Max drawdownMaximum Loss Limit $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500; trails the end-of-day balance (monitored in real time incl. unrealized P&L), never moves down, locks permanently at the starting balance in the Combine. XFA: starts at -$2,000/-$3,000/-$4,500, locks at $0; resets to $0 after the first payout.
Drawdown typeEOD trailing
Profit split90/10. 100% of the first $10,000 lifetime profit only for traders on the new dashboard before 12 Jan 2026.
Payout frequencyPath chosen at XFA activation. Standard: after 5 non-consecutive winning days of $150+; max 50% of balance, capped $2,000 / $3,000 / $5,000. Consistency: after 3 trading days with largest day <=40% of net profit; max 50% of balance, capped $3,000 / $4,000 / $6,000. Live Funded: 50% of balance, no cap; daily payouts after 30 winning Live days. Limited time (since 2 Jun 2026): adding the DLL at checkout on a new Combine doubles the per-request caps to $4,000 / $6,000 / $10,000 (Standard) and $6,000 / $8,000 / $12,000 (Consistency).
Payout minimum$125
Min trading daysNo fixed minimum; 55% consistency target means at least 2 days ('can pass in as few as two days')
Time limitNot stated; subscription continues until cancelled
Consistency ruleCombine: best day must stay at or below 55% of total profit (hard line, no buffer). XFA Standard: none. XFA Consistency path: largest day <=40% of net profit to be payout-eligible.
News tradingAllowed, but purposefully trading the full maximum position size directly into a scheduled major news event is prohibited; risk adjustments may apply to high-volatility products.
EAs and botsCustom bots allowed via the TopstepX/ProjectX API ($29/month) on Combine and XFA only, not Live; HFT prohibited; orders must originate from the trader's own device (no VPS/VPN for trading).
Weekend holdingNot allowed: all positions must be closed by 3:10 PM CT every weekday; no swing trading.
Copy tradingTopstepX Trade Copier allowed on Combine and XFA (not Live). Cross-account hedging, account stacking and coordinated trading with others are prohibited. Up to 5 active XFAs.
Leverage / contractsMax 5 minis / 50 micros (50K), 10 / 100 (100K), 15 / 150 (150K); XFA follows a balance-based Scaling Plan
PlatformsProjectX, Quantower
InstrumentsCME Group futures: equity index, FX, agricultural, energy, metals, interest rate (no spot forex)

Good to know

  • Trading Combine and Express Funded Account are simulated; the Live Funded Account is by invitation from the Risk Team.
  • Back2Funded: an XFA lost before its first payout can be reactivated within 30 days for $599 / $699 / $829 (50K / 100K / 150K), up to 2 times.
  • Payout fees: ACH and wire $30 each; Aeropay, Wise and prop-to-brokerage have no Topstep fee.
  • TopstepX cannot connect to TradingView; Quantower connects via TopstepX for Combine and XFA only.
  • Live Funded Account Daily Loss Limit starts at $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 and steps down when the tradable balance falls.

What we couldn't verify

  • No effective date published for the Combine consistency change from 50% (with buffer) to 55%.
  • Third-party trackers report a 28 Apr 2026 change to No Activation Fee pricing and XFA payout caps; no official announcement found, so it is not listed under changes.
  • NinjaTrader, Tradovate and Rithmic are not mentioned on current help pages; only TopstepX (ProjectX) and Quantower via TopstepX are confirmed.
  • Topstep Labs article does not publish the profit split, news or holding rules for the Static and Freedom Combines.
  • Several help articles show only relative update dates ('updated over 2 weeks ago').
