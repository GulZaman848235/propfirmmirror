Restricted countries

BrightFunded lists 6 restricted countries on its main list, used for its CFD programs.

Cuba Iran North Korea Pakistan Syria Vietnam

Applies to people residing in or holding nationality of these countries (help article dated 15 Sep 2026). Pakistan IS on the list: Pakistani traders cannot buy new challenges, but those with an already active account may continue trading. MT5 is not available to US or UAE citizens/residents; cTrader is not available to US citizens/residents; DXtrade has no listed country limit.
