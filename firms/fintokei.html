  1. 21 Oct 2026 CFD

    StartTrader becomes a 1-phase program with soft breach

    Accounts bought from 21 Oct 2026: 1 phase with 3% target, 2% daily loss (first hit is a soft breach), 8% max loss, no consistency rule, 80% flat reward, 2% minimum profit for payout, 60-day limit. Previously 3 phases, 3% daily / 6% max.

    Consistency Rules applied in two levels

    CR1: open-trade risk capped at 1% and the reward ratio cut by 15 points. CR2: 1% risk, a +1% daily profit cap, 1:10 FX / 1:5 other leverage, a 30-point reward cut and a frozen Loyalty Program. Usually applied after earlier warnings.

    Source: support.fintokei.com/en/articles/11315966-consistency-rules-most-common-application

Good to know

  • Accounts are available in USD, EUR, CZK and JPY; USD prices shown.
  • All programs have a 3% maximum risk on open trades; breaking it triggers warnings and possible consistency restrictions rather than an immediate fail.
  • Accounts close if no trade is opened in any 30-calendar-day period.
  • TradingView trading is via cTrader connection.
  • Martingale and aggressive averaging are allowed but not recommended (help centre).

What we couldn't verify

  • Founding year: homepage says 'Established in 2011' without naming the entity it refers to; left null.
  • Brand colour not identified.
