Good to know

  • A notional volume limit caps total open notional by account size (e.g. $1,000,000 on $50K, $2,000,000 on $200K).
  • Pro accounts accrue a monthly payment of 1% of purchased capital, accrued daily.
  • Withdrawals are processed every Friday via Finotive Pay; minimum request 1% of initial balance.
  • Terms say the Terms prevail over marketing pages and FAQs.
  • Finotive Futures (finotivefutures.com, Finotive Futures Technologies Limited, DIFC) launched as a separate futures brand; not yet covered here.

What we couldn't verify

  • No dated rule-change announcements found on official pages; Terms carry no visible effective date.
  • Time limits are not stated in the Terms we could read.
  • 2-Step Solo is not on the homepage program cards. Its sizes stay unverified because the help centre (LaunchPad window, placeholder price article) conflicts with Terms s. 10A.1, which put Solo in the standard catalogue; fees are from Terms s. 8.8.2 and match the public checkout.
