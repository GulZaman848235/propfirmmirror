Good to know

  • Futures: up to 5 simulated funded accounts per individual and per household (V2 Select 300K funded accounts can take the total to 8); up to 15 Select evaluations per 30 days.
  • Futures broker chosen at checkout (same price): Tradovate (NinjaTrader, TradingView), Rithmic (Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader) or WealthCharts. ProjectX is no longer offered.
  • Futures funded microscalping rule: over 50% of trades and of profits must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds.
  • Futures Elite Live: Tradeify may invite traders after 3 payouts on one account or 10 in total; 80/20 split, daily payouts.
  • Tradeify FX funded accounts are capped at $300,000 combined; payouts via Rise or Confirmo (USDC).
  • Accounts bought before 12 Sep 2025 keep older contract limits, payout tables and consistency levels.
  • tradeifycrypto.co redirects to Tradeify Perps, which showed only an early-access sign-up on 2026-10-30; no purchasable crypto program found.

What we couldn't verify

  • help.tradeify.co returned HTTP 403; the same articles were read on Tradeify's official Intercom mirror (intercom.help/tradeify).
  • A search snippet of help.tradeify.co mentions a separate tier of countries eligible for evaluations but not Live; that tier was not visible on the mirror.
  • Help articles show only relative update dates; dashboard-launch and other change dates not published.
  • Tradeify FX Daily plan minimum trading days not stated; Select 300K parameters not captured.
  • Copy trading between own futures accounts is not addressed in the futures help centre.
  • The firm also offers WealthCharts (its own broker connection) and Tradesea (via Rithmic), which are not in the site's platform list.
