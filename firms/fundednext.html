Good to know

  • CFD FundedNext Accounts cap risk at 3% of initial balance at any time, including floating losses.
  • Stellar challenges can pay a 15% 'challenge reward' on the profit target when Scale-Up criteria are met (not for US traders).
  • The payout option on Stellar 2-Step and Lite is chosen at checkout and cannot be changed later.
  • Futures Legacy accounts are no longer sold (help article says 'as of October 11', year not stated); existing Legacy accounts cannot be reset.
  • A FundedNext Labs futures experiment (FNL:003, $50K, $1,000 daily loss, $2,000 EOD trailing, max 3 accounts) is listed without a clear open/closed status.

  • CFD minimum payout and weekend-holding rules for Stellar 1-Step, 2-Step and Lite are not stated on the help articles opened.

  • Futures news, EA/bot, copy-trading and overnight rules come from search snippets of the Futures FAQ, not an opened page.
  • Help-centre search snippets say Rapid (original) and Bolt futures accounts stopped being sold from 10 July 2026; this was not confirmed on an opened page, so it is not listed as a change.
  • An earlier internal note (2026-10-23) said the site's data marked the CFD $100K as unavailable for Pakistan; not confirmed on any official page opened.
