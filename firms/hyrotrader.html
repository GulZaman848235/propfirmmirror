Good to know

  • One-Step and Two-Step fees are refundable deposits returned with the first payout; the Zero-Step fee is not refunded once the challenge is activated.
  • Aggregate limits: USD 200,000 in funded accounts and USD 400,000 in evaluations per person.
  • Also connects BloFin (API demo) and the Tealstreet and Tiger.com terminals, which are not in the site's platform list.
  • Martingale and cross-account hedging prohibited; low-cap coin exposure limited to 5% of initial balance.
  • At least one trade must be closed every 30 days, including on funded accounts, or the account closes.

What we couldn't verify

  • Price table comes from an official blog article (20 Oct 2026), not a live checkout page.
  • No maximum per-trade risk figure is published; leverage per pair is set by the connected platform (Challenge Terms Schedule 1 Part C), so the leverage figures come from the homepage.
  • Criteria for the discretionary 90% split are not published.
