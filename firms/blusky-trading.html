Good to know

  • Launch and Propel have three stages: Evaluation, Buffer Zone, then Sim Funded; failing the Buffer Zone resets only the Buffer Zone.
  • Hedging is prohibited at the user/household level.
  • Exceeding contract limits does not fail the account, but the system may cap size or liquidate.
  • Payouts are paid via Gusto or Rise; payouts must be claimed within 30 days of the request. Non-US traders submit a W-8BEN.
  • Sim Funded accounts that reach $10,000 total profit plus payouts must open a Brokerage Review ticket; a broker denial closes the account and forfeits simulated profits.
  • The firm also lists TradeSea, Tickblaze, BlackArrow, ATAS, DeepCharts and ONYX connections, which are not in this site's platform list.

  • Pricing tables load in a script, so most sizes have no readable price.   • Stock Plans: price, billing type and profit targets not published on readable pages.
