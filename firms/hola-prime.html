Restricted countries

Hola Prime lists 35 restricted countries.

Afghanistan Belarus Burundi Central African Republic China Congo Cuba DR Congo Eritrea Guinea Guinea-Bissau Haiti Hong Kong India Iran Iraq Laos Libya Mali Moldova Myanmar Nicaragua North Korea Oman Pakistan Russia Somalia Sri Lanka Sudan Syria Tunisia Ukraine Vietnam Yemen Zimbabwe

Big change. Hola Prime's FAQ 'Who is eligible to join Hola Prime?' now gives a full list of 32 countries, and PAKISTAN and INDIA are on it. The Futures FAQ has the same list (https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/about-hola-prime/who-is-eligible-to-join-hola-prime/), so there is no separate futures list. The older footer sentence is still on every page: 'does not provide services to the residents of certain countries including – Afghanistan, Belarus, Burundi, China, Cuba, Congo, Sudan, Sri Lanka, North Korea ... and Yemen'. Sudan (SD), Sri Lanka (LK) and 'Congo' appear only in that footer, and we keep them from it; 'Congo' stays as the Republic of the Congo (CG), as before. US clients are accepted, but MT5 and cTrader are not available to them. Forex Sim. Funded allocation is capped at $90K for Nigeria, Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Israel, Thailand, Botswana, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Jamaica
