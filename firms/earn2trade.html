Earn2Trade lists 25 restricted countries.

Good to know

  • After passing, traders choose LiveSim (simulated, paid withdrawals) or Live where available; partner prop firms decide on Live accounts.
  • Trader Career Path scales through larger funded accounts (TCP25 up to $200K; TCP50/TCP100 up to $400K) by withdrawing the profit target at each step.
  • LiveSim activation fee ($139, non-professional) is only charged from the first profitable withdrawal.
  • Up to 5 concurrent evaluations and 3 funded accounts (only one Live).
  • Also sells a Beginner Crash Course (education, not an evaluation).

What we couldn't verify

  • Official program pages render one size at a time; all prices were read on the purchase pages (TCP25 $150, TCP50 $190, TCP100 $350; GAU50 $170, GAU100 $315, GAU150 $375, GAU200 $550).
  • Evaluation profit targets, drawdowns and contract limits for TCP50, TCP100 and GAU100-200 are shown only in images; TCP rules are written for TCP25 and Gauntlet rules for GAU50.
  • Funded-account overnight/weekend holding rules and automated-trading (bot) policy are not stated explicitly.
