Restricted countries

City Traders Imperium lists 7 restricted countries.

Belarus Cuba Iran Myanmar North Korea Russia Syria

Section 7 of the AML Policy ('Prohibited Jurisdictions and Sanctions Compliance') gives this 'at a minimum' list for citizens or residents. It adds Myanmar (MM) and Belarus (BY) to the 5 countries already in our file. The site footer, the Terms page (https://citytradersimperium.com/terms-conditions-of-service/) and the homepage FAQ still name only North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba and Russia, plus anyone under international sanctions. Using a VPN or false documents to get around this leads to termination. Pakistan is not listed.
