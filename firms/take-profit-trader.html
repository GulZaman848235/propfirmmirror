Good to know

  • Account path: Test (simulated, no withdrawals) -> PRO (simulated, 80/20, withdrawals from day one) -> PRO+ (live market, 90/10).
  • Up to 5 PRO or PRO+ accounts at a time; up to 3 PRO resets.
  • The FAQ says a failed Test costs at most $360.
  • Terms: if you buy an account and change your mind, it is refunded within 72 hours if it has not been used or traded, minus a $75 fee.
