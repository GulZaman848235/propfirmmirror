Good to know

  • Up to 3 evaluation accounts and up to 3 funded accounts at once; only one may be an Express Funding account.
  • Express Funding: traders whose funded account was terminated in the past 24 hours can buy an Express account of the same size and re-qualify in 3 trading days; billed every 30 days, at most twice per 30-day period; its price table is an image on the help centre.
  • Funded accounts: trades held under 10 seconds are prohibited; withdrawals lower the balance but not the drawdown threshold; the firm's drawdown article mentions a probation check on net P&L at each 15-calendar-day interval during the first 90 funded days.
  • Rithmic R|Trader Pro and NinjaTrader are free once funded; exchange data fees for funded traders are covered by the funding partners.
  • Crypto withdrawals (BTC/USDT) carry a 5% processor fee; bank wires post in 1–3 business days.
  • Home page says 'over 20' execution platforms are supported; only those named by the firm are listed (also Trade Navigator and Quick Screen, not in the site's platform list).

What we couldn't verify

  • Whether automated trading/bots are allowed is not stated on official pages.
  • Activation fee for sizes above 50K and for Pro Evaluation is described only as the remaining 50% of the evaluation fee; exact amounts are not published as text.
  • Funded Dynamic Scaling contract limits are shown only in an image on the help centre and could not be read.
  • Funded drawdown amounts per size are not listed in text; the firm only states that the same real-time trailing rule (stopping at the starting balance) applies to funded accounts.
  • Founding year not stated (About page says 'more than a decade').
