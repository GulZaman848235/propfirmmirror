Good to know

  • The help centre says the firm is backed by the broker Moneta Markets.
  • Each program keeps the rules of the version that was current when the account was bought.
  • The old 1-Step (6% static max loss) is no longer sold. The help centre says the revised 1-Step and 1-Step Daily are sold alongside each other.
  • Funded 2-Step accounts: a floating loss of 3% on one symbol (2% on the lower-cost option) closes the trades on that symbol, and later risk per trade idea is then limited to 1.5%.
  • Instant Funding Pro: a 3% floating loss on one symbol triggers the same 1.5% limit.
  • Phoenix Scaling Program: 11 levels from $2,500 to $2,000,000; traders can start at Levels 1 to 4. Rules: 10% target to move up a level, 3% daily loss, 6% static max loss, 88% split, payouts every 14 days (minimum $100). No purchase prices are published.
  • Live Plan: invitation-only accounts with real capital. Traders on it can no longer buy new challenges.
  • Prohibited: martingale-style sizing, reverse trading, hedging or grid trading, latency arbitrage, tick scalping, high-frequency trading, and account sharing.
  • MT5 is not offered to US traders.

What we couldn't verify

  • Legal entity, registered address and founding year were not found: the help centre does not publish them, and the site footer and terms were blocked.
  • 1-Step availability: one help-centre page says the revised 1-Step and 1-Step Daily are sold alongside each other. Another says 1-Step Daily is the current one-step product and the 'legacy 1-Step' is not for sale. We list both.
  • The revised 1-Step article gives no payout minimum or time limit. The $100 minimum and unlimited time come from the earlier 1-Step page and the product overview.
  • Help-centre articles show only relative update times ('Updated over 2 weeks ago'). Change dates come from dates stated inside the articles.
  • The web-search quota was used up during this run, so pages could only be found through help-centre navigation.
  • Prices were read from the product data the homepage challenge builder loads (in a browser on 2026-10-30). Phoenix Instant's loss limits and split were not shown on the homepage card.
