Good to know

  • Evaluation trading is simulated; 'funds' in the evaluation are fictitious per the firm.
  • Trades must last at least 30 seconds (MAX/FLEX) and move at least $0.10 per share between entry and exit; opening size capped at 5% of the previous 1-minute candle's volume.
  • Day accounts are liquidated at 15:50 ET; no overnight holds through earnings; positions must be closed before stock splits and ex-dividend dates.
  • Platform is TraderEvolution-based and customised by the firm (Windows, web, iOS, Android).
  • Funded accounts scale at each 10% validated profit milestone (+5% buying power, +10% daily pause).

What we couldn't verify

  • Pricing cards do not label FLEX vs MAX columns; mapping inferred from the trading-period row (Unlimited = FLEX, 60/100 days = MAX), consistent with Program Terms.
  • Consistency figures on the pricing cards (30% for MAX) conflict with the FAQ (Day MAX none, Swing MAX 70%).
  • Weekend: Swing description allows weekend holding while the FAQ bars trading Fri 8pm-Mon 3am ET.
  • Max-loss type (static/trailing) not stated for FLEX/MAX; Program Terms say max drawdown moves to initial balance after 3x daily-loss in new profit.
  • Blog/announcements not checked for dated rule changes (web search budget exhausted).
