Good to know

  • CFD scaling plan: 10% profit in each of 3 consecutive months increases the funded balance by 50%, up to $5 million.
  • All CFD and futures accounts fail after 30 consecutive days without trading.
  • A Zero Activation Fee 2-phase challenge ($19 entry, 8% / 5% targets, activation fee $219-$599 on pass) is described as a limited pre-launch offer in selected regions only; not listed as a standard program.
  • FunderPro Futures platforms: WebTrader (browser), DeepCharts, DeepMap, Quantower and ATAS (own licence); platform choice is final after purchase.
  • FunderPro Futures permits martingale and scalping; positions must be closed before the weekend and scheduled holidays.

What we couldn't verify

  • Founding year not stated on official pages.
  • Minimum trading days for Pro and One Phase challenges shown as '-' in the official spec table; not explicitly confirmed as 'none'.
  • Instant Program platforms not stated separately; assumed same as challenges (MT5, cTrader, TradeLocker).
  • Futures leverage is not a ratio; contract limits (5/10/15/20 standard contracts by size) are shown for Beginner and Professional, not yet for Mid-Level.
  • No dated CFD rule-change announcements in the last 12 months found on funderpro.com (news section posts are from 2023-2025).
