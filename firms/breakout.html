Good to know

  • Kraken runs three related prop products on shared infrastructure: Breakout (standalone platform), Kraken Prop (inside Kraken Pro; plans named Advanced / Intermediate / Starter with the same 9%/3%, 12%/5% and 10%/6% target/drawdown pairs) and Kraken Funded (mobile app; 12% target, 3% static drawdown, no daily loss limit, accounts up to $10,000).
  • Maximum $200,000 in combined active evaluation sizes at any time.
  • Trading fee 0.04% per side on Breakout.
  • Kraken Prop payouts require no open positions or orders and are credited in USD or USDG to the Kraken wallet.
  • Index and commodity markets are listed by the firm alongside crypto; the site's markets field shows crypto only.

  • Whether a 2-Step evaluation is still sold is unclear: an official comparison article says it was discontinued in May 2026, while the checkout page still mentions 1-step or 2-step.
