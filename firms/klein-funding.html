Restricted countries

Klein Funding does not publish a restricted-country list we could find. Ask the firm before you buy.

The site renders in headless Chrome (plain curl gets 403). The Bybit page (https://kleinfunding.com/bybit-prop-firm) says 'No banned countries — trade from anywhere Bybit is unavailable too.' The FAQ at https://kleinfunding.com/faqs/which-countries-can-participate-in-klein-funding-crypto welcomes traders 'from all around the world' who are 18 or older. The homepage FAQ 'Which Countries Are Restricted from Using Klein Funding?' contradicts this: it says a restricted list is kept on the website and points to the Terms of Use. But neither the Terms of Use (https://kleinfunding.com/terms-of-use) nor the AML Policy (https://kleinfunding.com/aml-policy) has a country list. Disclaimer and pricing pages checked too. Pakistan is not mentioned.
