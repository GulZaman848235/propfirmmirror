Good to know

  • Anti-gambling policy prohibits one-sided bets, aggressive averaging, martingale, tick scalping, grid trading and execution flooding.
  • Funded accounts may be closed after 365 days of inactivity; challenge accounts after 90 days.
  • Payouts can also be transferred to a Taurex broker account.
  • The firm states it is not licensed or regulated by a financial authority and all trading is in a demo environment.
