Good to know

  • Accounts run only on the ThinkMarkets app (ThinkTrader), which integrates with TradingView.
  • An account fails if no new trade is opened for 30 days.
  • Lightning, Dual Step and Nexus funded accounts need 3 profitable days (+0.5% each, balance above start) per payout cycle.
  • Payout methods are not available to US residents (homepage).

What we couldn't verify

  • Challenge time limit not stated on pages checked.
  • Bolt leverage: FAQ says up to 1:100 on forex, homepage card says dynamic up to 1:50.
  • Founding year not stated.
  • Base profit split for Lightning/Dual Step/Nexus inferred from the homepage calculator (80%, 90% with add-on); program cards only say 'up to 90%'.
