Good to know

  • Standard plans and Instant: at least 50% of trades must be held 60 seconds or longer. 2-Step Omo and Instant Mini funded trades must be held at least 150 seconds or the profit is removed.
  • Platform can be changed only before the account has been traded.
  • KYC must be completed before any payout.
  • Terms and Conditions were last amended 23 Oct 2026 (version 2026.2); the page does not list what changed.
