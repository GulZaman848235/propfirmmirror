Good to know

  • Monthly subscription; a reset is a separate fee and does not change the 30-day billing date.
  • Up to 10 accounts at once, up to 5 Funded Sim and up to 5 Funded Live; all accounts must be on the same broker (Tradovate/CQG or Rithmic).
  • NinjaTrader Desktop is not supported on Rithmic accounts. Rithmic-side platforms also include ATAS, EdgeProX, Finamark, Volfix, WealthCharts, Multicharts and Overcharts.
  • Quick Pay End of Day: the evaluation drawdown is end-of-day, but the Funded Sim drawdown is intraday.
  • Funded Sim and Funded Live require KYC/AML checks.
  • Subscription fees are non-refundable except where required by law.

What we couldn't verify

  • No daily loss limit is listed on the pricing cards or rules articles, and the firm does not explicitly say 'none'; dailyLoss left null.
  • Fast Pass minimum days: pricing card and rules article say 3 trading days, but the 29 May 2026 TradeDay 2.0 post said Fast Pass removes the minimum trading-day requirement.
  • Quick Pay has no maximum payout stated in the payout policy.
  • Help-centre article dates are shown without a year, so they could not be used to date rule changes.
  • The pricing cards show a running 55%/50% discount; the struck-through figures are used as list prices.
